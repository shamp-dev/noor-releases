# Noor 1.2.0

## What's new

**Noor is free**
Prayer times and the prayer tracker, the Quran with translations, tafsir and recitation, the Hijri calendar, tasbih, Qibla and Comfort for every feeling are all free.

**Noor Premium** adds:
- Learn Arabic: 16 lessons and 12 stories of the prophets
- Ask Noor: answers about Islam grounded in the Quran and Sunnah
- Safe browsing: blocks adult websites across your whole phone
- No ads

**Ads in the free version**
- A small banner on the Prayers, Calendar, Tasbih and Quran list screens
- An occasional full-screen ad after closing a surah or finishing your after-salah tasbih
- Never while you read the Quran, or in tafsir, recitation or Comfort (feelings and duas)
- Family-friendly ads only. Premium and invite-code members never see ads.
- Where required by law, you can change your ad privacy choices in Settings.

**Smaller and faster**
- Much smaller download
- Smoother Prayers screen and tasbih counter
- Faster opening of long surahs at a saved ayah

**Fixes**
- Text fields (like passwords) no longer hide behind the keyboard

## Install
1. Download **noor.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser to "install unknown apps".
3. Already have Noor? Install over it. Your account and progress stay.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
